Quitting Salaryman Life

A Week With Zero Income in Tokyo After Quitting My Job

Yudai · August 11, 2026 · 7 min read

The day I decided to quit, I barely had any savings. I gave up a steady paycheck and the title on my business card, and decided to spend a week in Tokyo starting from zero.

On day one, counting what was left in my wallet was honestly nerve-wracking. Still, I felt like I was finally getting to define the word "freedom" for myself.

▶

What Became Visible After Giving Up Stability

As a company employee, I thought the packed morning trains and meetings spent reading my boss's mood were just "normal." But once I quit, I realized how much that "normal" had been wearing me down.

"Only after my income hit zero did I finally see what I'd actually been spending money on — and what I truly valued."

By the end of the week, what remained wasn't just anxiety. It was the feeling that I could choose my next step on my own terms — and maybe that was the thing I'd been looking for all along.
