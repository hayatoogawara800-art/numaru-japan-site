We're fixing up an old farmhouse in Izu.

You're invited before it's finished.

A thatched-roof house on the coast of the Izu Peninsula, about three hours south of Tokyo. We're turning it into a place where travelers, surfers and the people who actually live here end up in the same room.

It's not open yet. Here's the plan — and what you can already come and do with us.

What we're building

Not a hotel. Not a cheap dorm. Something in between, that we couldn't find when we needed it.

1. A base, not a bed.

Stay three nights or three months. A kitchen you can actually cook in, a desk you can work at, a place where you unpack instead of living out of your bag.

2. A room where you meet people.

Travelers, surfers, remote workers — and the locals we've spent years getting to know. Dinners, barbecues, a beer on the floor. Nobody eats alone here.

3. A way into Shimoda.

Shimoda is easy to visit and hard to get into. Almost nothing is in English. We're the door: we translate, we introduce, we know who to call.

What you can actually do

The house isn't ready, but Izu is. These are things we can do with you today — not a wish list.

Available now

Surf

Beginner-friendly beach breaks, and we come in with you.

Shimoda has some of the cleanest, most forgiving beaches within reach of Tokyo. If you've never stood up on a board, this is a good place to try it. If you've surfed for years, we'll take you to the right beach for the day's swell.

Year-round (wetsuit Nov–May) · Half a day · Beginners welcome · Board rental arranged

Available now

Walk the coast and the hills

The parts of Izu that don't show up on the tourist map.

Cliff paths above the water, quiet forest roads, small shrines nobody visits, and beaches you'll have to yourself. We walk them with you and tell you what you're looking at.

Year-round · 1–4 hours · Easy to moderate · No gear needed

Available now

Eat and drink with locals

This is the one we actually care about.

Dinner at someone's place, a barbecue by the water, or a night in a tiny izakaya where you're the only foreigner in the room and it doesn't matter, because we're translating. Fishermen, farmers, surf shop owners, people who moved here from the city. This is how Shimoda stops being a place you visited.

Year-round · Evenings · Small groups · We translate

Available now

Help us fix the house

Come back in two years and point at a wall you built.

Right now the work is unglamorous: clearing, demolition, hauling, scrubbing, painting. No skills required, and you won't be doing it alone. It's the fastest way we know to stop being a tourist and start being someone who was there.

Year-round · Half or full day · No experience needed · Gloves and tools provided

The house, step by step

We're not going to pretend this is finished. Here's exactly where we are.

Phase 01 We are here

An empty house and a lot of dust

Thatched roof, wooden frame, decades of someone else's life still inside it. First job is emptying it out and finding out what's actually holding it up.

You can join: clearing, hauling, demolition.

Phase 02

Roof, frame, floors

The unsexy money. Everything that decides whether this building is still standing in fifty years.

You can join: watching real craftsmen work, which is worth more than it sounds.

Phase 03

Rooms to sleep in

Beds, a bathroom that works, hot water, wifi that doesn't drop. The point where people can actually stay.

You can join: the first nights, before anything is polished.

Phase 04

The big room

The heart of it. One long table, a kitchen, a place where fifteen people can eat together and nobody has to leave at ten.

You can join: the first dinner.

Phase 05

Open

You can join: come back.

Getting here

From Tokyo — one train, no transfers.

The Odoriko limited express runs from Tokyo Station straight to Izukyu-Shimoda. Roughly 2 hours 45 minutes. Or take the Shinkansen to Atami and switch to the Izukyu line.

When to come.

Summer is the beach and it's busy. Autumn is the best weather of the year and almost empty. Winter is cold, clear, and the surf is good. Spring is green and quiet. There is no bad month, only different ones.
